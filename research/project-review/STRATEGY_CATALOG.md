## Shared offline implementation: S01–S05

Evidence: [M1 load_data/signals](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/engine.py:48), [replay](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/engine.py:118), [M5 aggregation](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/m5/engine.py:48), [registered request](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/request.json:19), [combined selection](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/combined.py:26), [offline dispatch](E:/GPT/Inves/research/backtest-only-20261010/cycle.py:57).

Seven symbols means XAUUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF, USDCAD, AUDUSD. Both directions are implemented. M5 registers S01–S03 only; its copied signals function also contains S04/S05 branches, but those branches are not in its registered request. M5 is a parameter/timeframe variant for S01–S03, not an independent strategy family.

Data: hashed broker NPZ structured arrays with time, OHLC, spread, tick/real volume and saved contract metadata. Broker time is converted by the manifest's offset, historically assumed 180 minutes. M5 accepts only five consecutive, aligned M1 candles. ATR is a trailing arithmetic mean of 14 true ranges, not Wilder ATR. EMA20/EMA60 seed from the first close. Previous 20-bar highs/lows exclude the signal candle. First 100 bars and 100 bars following each timestamp gap cannot signal; EMA state continues across gaps rather than reinitializing. Missing data are not interpolated. Sessions: ALL or EU_US, latter signal-close UTC hour ≥7 and <20 after the assumed offset conversion.

Notation: O/H/L/C = completed signal candle, A = ATR14, B = (C−O)/A, D = (EMA20−EMA60)/A, U/V = maximum high/minimum low of previous 20 bars, q = bodyATR threshold. Registered grid: q ∈ {0.2, 0.45}; minStopATR ∈ {0.5, 1.0}; session ∈ {ALL, EU_US}; targetR=2. There is no single family default: frozen plan model objects choose the values. replay arguments override spread multiplier (default 1), fee per lot (7) and delay bars (0). Current manager config does not override these offline rules. targetR is hardcoded to 2 in rr_tp, despite being present as plan metadata.

Timing: signals use completed candles, entry at next available consecutive bar's open (plus optional delay). No intrabar signal or forming candle. Entry spread uses d.spread[i], itself 1.25×max prior five completed bar spreads with a minimum one tick. Thus it deliberately excludes the signal bar's spread; subsequent exit bar k uses d.spread[k−1]. This additional lag must be preserved or explicitly versioned, not silently “corrected.” M5 uses previous five M5 maximum spreads (up to 25 minutes), contrary to copied request prose saying five M1 bars.

Risk/exits: one position per symbol per replay, broker volume_min, no account starting-capital ledger, no leverage or aggregate margin/portfolio exposure check, no cooldown or daily loss halt. Entry cost adds half a tick adversely. SL is beyond the previous three bars including the signal candle by two ticks; distance at least max(minStopATR×A, 4×modeled spread, broker stop distance). Sell SL includes modeled spread. Prices round outward. TP produces at least 2R gross USD reward, using inverse-price conversion for USDJPY/USDCHF/USDCAD. Spread/A >0.25 skips entry. No trailing or timed exit. Stop gaps fill at worse of stop/open plus two adverse ticks; target never improves; both-hit candle is SL first. Unclosed positions at partition end are censored, omitted from win rate, and not liquidated. Simulation advances beyond exit before searching again. Fee=7 USD/lot round trip by default; swap scenario=10 USD/lot per broker midnight, not actual fees or financing.

Arbitration is research selection, not simultaneous signal arbitration: engine.grid evaluates presets independently. Validation score=expectancyR−0.15×DD_R/n, with n<50 assigned −1e6. Combined selection prefers provisional gate, then score, then M1 tie; one selected model per symbol. Fixed-R sums are not a portfolio equity curve. Current code can regenerate colliding M1/M5 IDs, but saved plans use OR1 versus OR2M5 and have zero collisions. Freeze those saved plans and hashes; do not regenerate them with current plan().

### S01 — Range rejection

Purpose: mean reversion at a prior range extreme in a weak-trend regime. [signals branch](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/engine.py:77). Long iff |D|<0.25 AND L≤V+0.1A AND C>V+0.2A AND B≥q. Short iff |D|<0.25 AND H≥U−0.1A AND C<U−0.2A AND B≤−q. The common validity/session masks also apply. No separate higher-timeframe filter. Source, inputs, warm-up, timing, controls, configuration precedence, tests and limitations are exactly the shared implementation above. Complete and registered in M1/M5; ready for a frozen offline diagnostic. No unexplained signal component remains; execution realism remains limited.

### S02 — Trend pullback

Purpose: continuation after touching EMA20 in an EMA20/60 trend. [branch](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/engine.py:74). Long iff D>0.35 AND L≤EMA20+0.1A AND C>EMA20 AND B≥q. Short iff D<−0.35 AND H≥EMA20−0.1A AND C<EMA20 AND B≤−q. Strict trend thresholds and completed-bar EMA values matter. Common rules above apply. Complete, registered M1/M5, with independently poor saved selected outcomes; ready only under existing frozen plan, not a claim of positive expectancy.

### S03 — Break and retest

Purpose: delayed continuation beyond previous 20-bar structure. [state machine](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/engine.py:80). With no active level and no signal: bullish breakout C>U+0.05A AND B≥0.3 stores U/long; bearish C<V−0.05A AND B≤−0.3 stores V/short. On a subsequent valid bar within five bars, long retest iff L≤level+0.1A AND C>level+0.05A AND B≥q; short iff H≥level−0.1A AND C<level−0.05A AND B≤−q. Expire after >5 bars; invalid data clears the level. No explicit close-back-through-level invalidation before expiry. Trigger consumes level. Common next-bar entry and exits apply. Complete registered M1/M5; not the same as S09's event range or S17's exact Pro BTB.

### S04 — M1 momentum proxy

Purpose: simple bar continuation comparator. [branch](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/engine.py:71). Long iff B≥q AND C>previous C; short iff B≤−q AND C<previous C. Common masks and risk/exits apply; no five-second ticks or M1/tick agreement. Registered M1 only. Ready for a bar diagnostic; cannot validate S06's live limit-fill behavior.

### S05 — SP2L agent interpretation

Purpose: two-bar impulse followed by compressed countertrend structure and renewed break. [branch](E:/GPT/Inves/research/overnight-20261010/engine.py:92). For each valid i and count=2…5, start=i−count−2; spike=[start,start+1], micro=[start+2,i). For side s∈{+1,−1}: s×B_i≥q; each spike s×(C−O)≥0.65×(H−L); impulse=s×(C_start+1−O_start)≥2×ATR_start−1; each micro range≤0.7×mean spike range; long micro highs strictly decrease, short lows strictly increase. Long requires min micro lows≥spike last close−0.5×impulse and C_i>H_i−1+tick. Short requires max micro highs≤spike last close+0.5×impulse and C_i<L_i−1−tick. First qualifying count/side wins. No P-Gap or 0.75 close-location rule. Common exits/sizing apply. Complete registered M1; source-method fidelity expressly absent. Ready to test this numerical hypothesis only.
