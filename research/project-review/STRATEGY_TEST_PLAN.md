## Bias and correctness checks proposed after selection

1. Prefix invariance: append future data and assert earlier indicators/signals unchanged. Test bar-boundary clocks, tick timestamps, intrabar references and one-bar delay. Freeze signal information timestamps and require entry time≥information cutoff.
2. Stateful cases: S03 breakout expiry and gaps; S05 count/side ordering; S06 tick reference age, stop-side rounding, native currency reward and expiry/fill race; S07 first-trigger consumption; S08 partial buckets; S09 news reset, anchor rollover and first-confirmation failure. Do not infer pass from successful parsing.
3. Data leakage: verify previous-range excludes current bar, spread windows use only available information, training-only parameter selection, no timezone conversion using future broker metadata. A later quote/current contract snapshot cannot establish historical executability.
4. Repainting: compare event-time signal snapshots against batch results and prefix truncations. Fixed finalized bars are required; never promote a forming candle into a completed one by advancing a saved timestamp.
5. Survivorship/selection: record the explicit seven-symbol universe and all exclusions; conclusions apply to those contracts only. Include delisted/stale contracts only with valid historical metadata if expanding scope later. Count all448 historical searches and all newly tried variants; do not call each cost scenario a new independent sample.
6. Chronological splits: development/train first, validation second, untouched OOS last. Purge open trades at boundaries and show censored positions separately; also propose marked-to-market boundary sensitivity because dropping unresolved trades can distort win rate. Three expanding walk-forward folds are justified for S01–S05 given prior model search; train-only selection within each fold. No new optimization before user selection.
7. Intrabar: default SL-first if both bracket prices touch in one candle; report best/worst ordering bounds. Use actual bid/ask ticks for adjudication where complete, never assume candle-path order. Missing ticks mean unverified, not a win. Mark delayed-open positions and gap exits.
8. Accounting: independent net PnL reconciliation including commission/financing/FX conversion, realized and marked-to-market drawdown, pending margin reservation and simultaneous correlated USD exposure. R-denominator always original risk, not a moved stop.

## Proposed metrics, benchmarks and decision rules

Benchmarks: cash/no-trade net0 before interest assumptions; matched-cost simple momentum S04 for the bar families; paired always-eligible fixed-direction controls only as explicitly registered future diagnostics, not invented deployable strategies. Buy-and-hold is not a natural leveraged intraday FX benchmark without financing/margin definition. Report net/gross expectancyR, netUSD at stated size, PF, win rate including flats in denominator, TP-hit rate, drawdown both closed and mark-to-market, exposure time, turnover, cost/risk, holding time, independent entry days, rejected signals, censoring and both-hit frequency. Report Wilson intervals for win rate and day-block bootstrap intervals for expectancy; do not treat correlated trades as independent. Small samples are descriptive.

Proposal for review gate: at least100 closed OOS trades and20 independent entry days per nominated primary strategy/symbol (for event strategies, at least20 independent events plus adequate trades); net expectancy>0 and day-block95% lower bound>0, PF≥1.10 after base costs, acceptable predeclared mark-to-market drawdown, no correctness violations, and no reversal of conclusion under plausible cost stress. Drawdown threshold for a capital portfolio must be selected before testing; none is silently inherited from an unrelated demo waiver. These exploratory gates are not a profitability guarantee and do not authorize live/paper trading. If sample or cost evidence is insufficient, verdict remains inconclusive. A user's strategy selection alone does not authorize optimization or paper trading unless explicitly included.
