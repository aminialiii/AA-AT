# Prospective DEMO evaluation protocol

Status: proposed before any campaign trade. This document is separate from historical optimization and from educational rules. It defines reporting and ranking; it does not change any original strategy signal, stop, target, size, or management rule.

**Unit of analysis.** A trade is one fully closed, broker-reconciled strategy position with opening and closing order/deal IDs, frozen version/Magic, actual fill prices and volumes, complete cost fields, and raw broker payload hashes. Partial exits remain open until the entire position is reconciled. Same-symbol strategies sharing a netting position cannot be assigned independent trades and are blocked. Every attempted signal, skip, rejected order, late fill, suspension and connectivity gap is logged separately. Genuine signals may be sparse; there is no time deadline that forces 20 trades.

**Capital and PnL.** Report account-currency gross PnL, commission, swap/financing and fees, then net PnL reconciled to deals and balance changes. Initial R uses the broker-verified initial stop and actual entry volume/fill, with conversion through native broker profit calculation. Never recalculate R from a later trailed stop. Drawdown uses marked-to-market equity when trustworthy quotes exist; also report closed-equity drawdown separately. Missing fees, conversion, balance adjustments or marks are marked unknown and prevent a complete metric. Virtual strategy capital must be allocated and frozen before a shared-account run; portfolio margin and exposure are measured on the actual account.

**Twenty-trade screen.** For each strategy report n, elapsed real time, sessions and trading days, net PnL, R expectancy, win rate with Wilson interval, mean win/loss, R and account-currency profit factors, maximum marked-to-market and closed-equity drawdown, average/median duration, maximum consecutive losses, costs and slippage versus contemporaneous bid/ask, rejected/late/partial orders, downtime and reconciliation completeness. Report day-block bootstrap intervals for mean R when enough distinct days exist; when too few days exist, show the interval as unavailable rather than pretending trades are independent. Record regime tags from information available at entry (trend/range, volatility, spread/liquidity, session and news where source data is available). Show counts in each regime and suppress strong subgroup claims from small cells.

**Ranking and classes.** Before looking at outcomes, exclude `BLOCKED` or <20 verified closed trades from performance ranking. For eligible strategies, rank first by whether net R expectancy after actual costs is positive; then by smaller maximum drawdown and fewer risk/operational incidents; then by steadier risk-adjusted daily returns and better execution quality; use total dollar PnL only as a secondary descriptor. Show the complete metric vector rather than hiding trade-offs in one score. `PROMISING` requires ≥20 verified trades, positive observed net R and net account PnL, no hard risk breach, complete cost/reconciliation evidence, and acceptable execution quality; it only means extended forward testing is warranted. `UNDERPERFORMING` means ≥20 verified trades with negative observed net expectancy after costs. `INCONCLUSIVE` covers an insufficient sample, uncertain costs, conflicting metrics, or unstable evidence. `BLOCKED` means no safe/executable independent version. Intervals and the number of tested strategies accompany every rank; no claim of a statistically proven edge follows from 20 trades.

**Further evidence.** A candidate for extended DEMO observation needs a separately frozen duration and sample target, plus ongoing cost/regime reporting. Any mid-campaign rule edit receives a new candidate ID and its own ledger; past trades remain under the old version. Historical S01–S18 research and the 32 educational files may motivate questions but cannot replace a forward fill. Source provenance labels (`DIRECT_TRANSCRIPT`, `OFFICIAL_COMPANION_CLAIM`, `RECONSTRUCTION`, `UNVERIFIED`, `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`) remain attached to educational alignment notes; no teaching claim is treated as verified execution evidence.
