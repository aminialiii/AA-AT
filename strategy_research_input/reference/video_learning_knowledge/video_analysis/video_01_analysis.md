When you close half the position at `3R`, your banked profit (`0.5 * 3R = +1.5R`) is **three times** the remaining risk on the open half (`0.5 * -1R = -0.5R`) even if the Stop-Loss is kept at its original level! Thus, even if the market completely reverses and hits your original SL, you walk away with a net `+1.0R` profit (`+0.5R` net if half-closed at `2R`).     $$	ext{Realized PnL} = +0.5 \cdot k \cdot R$$

  - Unlike moving SL to breakeven (`Risk Free`), which frequently gets wicked out by normal pullbacks before continuation, `Half Close` at `2R`/`3R` allows the original structural Stop-Loss to remain intact while mathematically guaranteeing a net winning trade!
