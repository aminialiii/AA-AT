- **Spoken Explanation**:
  1. **Market Cycles & Candle-by-Candle Reading (`01:08:05` & Official Spec)**: First identify the active Market Cycle (`سایکل بازار`: Spike, Channel, or Trading Range). Inside that cycle, do NOT read price lazily "wave by wave" (`نه به صورت موج به موج`); read it **Candle by Candle (`کندل به کندل`)** and perform exact **Candle Counting (`کندل‌شماری`)** (`فوت کوزه‌گری ته کار همینه`).
  2. **Valid Spike Definition & The `P-Gap` Requirement (`شرط اعتبار اسپایک`)**:
     - A **Spike (`اسپایک`)** is a sudden, high-momentum directional burst consisting of one or more strong candles closing near their highs (in an uptrend) or lows (in a downtrend).
     - **Mandatory Validity Condition (`شرط اعتبار`)**: A sharp move is ONLY a valid Spike in `SP2L` if it creates a **Price Gap (`P-Gap` / `گپ قیمتی`)** between candles! (In classic price action / ICT terms, a `P-Gap` / micro-gap exists when Candle $i+1$'s Low does not overlap Candle $i-1$'s High in a bullish spike: $L_{i+1} > H_{i-1}$, leaving an unmitigated momentum gap across Candle $i$).
     - **Invalidation Rule**: *"If a sharp move forms WITHOUT a Price Gap (`بدون گپ`), it is NOT valid in the SP2L strategy."* (`اگر حرکت شارپ شکل بگیرد اما بدون گپ باشد، در این استراتژی معتبر محسوب نمی‌شود`).
- **Important Visual Information**: Annotated candlestick diagrams showing a valid bullish/bearish Spike containing a clear `P-Gap` (`گپ قیمتی`) between candles vs. an invalid overlapping move without a `P-Gap`.
- **Technical Details**:
  - **Bullish `P-Gap` Condition**: Across consecutive impulse candles $k-1, k, k+1$ (or a multi-candle spike from origin $k_0$ to peak $k_p$), there exists at least one non-overlapping price gap:
    $$L_{k+1} > H_{k-1} \quad (	ext{Bullish P-Gap})$$
  - **Bearish `P-Gap` Condition**:
    $$H_{k+1} < L_{k-1} \quad (	ext{Bearish P-Gap})$$
  - **Bullish Spike Continuity**: As long as each subsequent candle $j$ maintains $L_j \ge L_{j-1}$ (`لو بالاتر`), the first leg (Spike) is still in progress.

- **Prerequisites**: Ability to inspect individual candle Highs and Lows (`H_i, L_i, O_i, C_i`).
- **Expected Results**: Filtering out weak, overlapping retail momentum traps that lack institutional order-flow displacement.
- **Warnings**: Never trade `SP2L` on a move that lacks a `P-Gap`, no matter how large the candles look at a glance.

### `VIDEO_04_SEGMENT_004` — Hunting the 2nd Leg (`لگ دوم اسپایک`): Exact Pullback Trigger, Entry Level, SL, TP & 50% Scale-In
- **Video Identifier**: `VIDEO_04` (`7HEC5mO3d3U`)
- **Start Timestamp**: `00:20:00`
- **End Timestamp**: `00:38:00`
- **Topic**: Complete mathematical rules of `SP2L` (`Spike–2Leg`): how the 1st Pullback triggers the 2nd Leg, exact Entry Level, Stop-Loss placement, default `1:1` (and `1:2`) Take-Profit, and the **50% Stop-Distance Scale-In (`ورود تکمیلی روی ۵۰٪ فاصله ورود تا استاپ`)**.
- **Spoken Explanation**: `SP2L` stands for **Spike – Two Leg (`اسپایک دو لگ`)**, built on the universal price-action rhythm: **Power (`قدرت / اسپایک`) $ightarrow$ Pullback (`اصلاح`) $ightarrow$ Continuation (`ادامه / لگ دوم`)**.
  1. **Triggering the Pullback & 2nd Leg (`لگ دوم اسپایک`)**:
     - **In a Bullish Spike (`روند صعودی`)**: Count candles upward as long as each candle makes a higher low (`L_i > L_{i-1}`). Wait until a pullback candle **breaks below the Low of the previous candle (`منتظر می‌مانیم تا کندل اصلاحی کف کندل قبلی را بزند`: $L_t < L_{t-1}$)**.
     - **In a Bearish Spike (`روند نزولی`)**: Count candles downward as long as each candle makes a lower high (`H_i < H_{i-1}`). Wait until a pullback candle **breaks above the High of the previous candle (`سقف کندل قبلی را بزند`: $H_t > H_{t-1}$)**.
  2. **Primary Entry Level (`سطح ورود — Level`)**:
     - Once the pullback condition triggers ($L_t < L_{t-1}$ in bullish spike) and returns into the Spike's `P-Gap` / pullback zone, enter **in the direction of the Spike** (`Buy` after bullish spike; `Sell` after bearish spike) at the `SP2L` entry level (broken prior candle low / `P-Gap` pullback edge or bullish confirmation close).
  3. **Stop-Loss Placement (`حد ضرر — SL`)**:
     - Placed **behind the candle from which the Spike originated (`پشت کندلی که اسپایک از آن آغاز شده است`)**:
       - Bullish `SP2L`: $SL = L_{	ext{spike\_origin}} - 	ext{Spread\_Buffer}$
       - Bearish `SP2L`: $SL = H_{	ext{spike\_origin}} + 	ext{Spread\_Buffer}$
  4. **Take-Profit Target (`حد سود — TP`)**:
     - **Default TP**: Risk-to-Reward **`1:1` (`ریسک به ریوارد ۱:۱`)** (and extendable to `1:2` / `1:3` when trading with higher-timeframe trend or scaling in).
  5. **Supplementary 50% Scale-In Entry (`ورود تکمیلی روی ۵۰٪ فاصله`)**:
     - In addition to the primary entry ($P_{	ext{entry,1}}$), a **Supplementary Entry (`ورود تکمیلی`)** can be placed at the **50% midpoint between the primary Entry and the Stop-Loss (`روی ۵۰٪ فاصله نقطه ورود تا استاپ‌لاس`)**:
       $$P_{	ext{entry,2}} = rac{P_{	ext{entry,1}} + P_{	ext{SL}}}{2}$$
     - Adding a second position at the 50% pullback depth improves the average entry price (`میانگین ورود را بهبود داده`) and boosts effective R/R (since the 50% scale-in entry has half the stop distance of the primary entry, a move back to the primary `1:1` target yields **`3:1` R/R** on the scale-in leg!).
- **Important Visual Information**: Official `SP2L` structural schematic showing: Bullish Spike with `P-Gap`, Pullback candle sweeping prior candle's Low, Primary Entry Level, Supplementary 50% Scale-In Level, Stop-Loss behind Spike origin candle, and `1:1` / `1:2` Take-Profit targets.
- **Technical Details**:
  - Let $P_1 = P_{	ext{entry,1}}$, $S = P_{	ext{SL}}$, and $\Delta = |P_1 - S|$.
  - Primary Trade (Entry at $P_1$, SL at $S$, TP at $P_1 \pm \Delta$): Risk $= \Delta$, Reward $= \Delta$ (`1:1 R/R`).
  - Supplementary Scale-In Trade (Entry at $P_2 = P_1 \mp 0.5\Delta$, SL at $S$, TP at $P_1 \pm \Delta$):
    - Stop distance $= 0.5\Delta$.
    - Target distance to primary TP $= 1.5\Delta$.
    - Effective R/R of Supplementary Entry $= 1.5\Delta / 0.5\Delta = \mathbf{3.0R}$!

  - `Optimal_Timeframes = M1, M5` (applicable across all timeframes).
