# DEMO evaluation protocol addendum: 100-trade screen

This addendum records the 2026-10-22 target change from 20 to 100 fully closed, broker-reconciled DEMO trades per strategy. It does not rewrite the earlier [prospective protocol](../research/demo-forward-20261021/EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md), original strategy rules, or pre-change trade evidence. The account-bound configuration was versioned before the target migration.

Only genuine real-time broker orders and deals with opening and closing IDs, verified DEMO mode, frozen strategy version, actual fills, initial risk, and reconciled costs count. Historical research, replay, synthetic fills, and partially open positions are excluded. The nominal 1,800-trade goal applies only if all eighteen strategies become executable; five currently are, for a 500-trade eligible target. No deadline or manual trade forcing applies.

At 100 closed trades, report net PnL after recorded costs, expectancy in initial R, profit factor, win rate with Wilson interval, mean win and loss, marked-to-market and closed-trade drawdown separately, duration, consecutive losses, execution latency and slippage, spread/commission/swap impact, rejection and suspension rates, distinct trading days, and regime/session counts. If cost or equity-mark evidence is incomplete, label that metric unavailable. Use day-block uncertainty intervals only when enough independent days exist. Compare all tested strategies transparently and account for selection bias; do not infer a durable edge from 100 trades.

Classification is conservative: fewer than 100 valid trades is `INCONCLUSIVE`; missing cost, execution-quality, drawdown, or regime evidence also leaves a positive observed result `INCONCLUSIVE`. A negative observed net expectancy at 100 or more may be `UNDERPERFORMING` for this screen. `PROMISING` requires positive net R and account-currency results plus complete cost/reconciliation evidence, no hard risk breach, acceptable execution quality and drawdown, and enough independent market observations to justify extended DEMO validation. `BLOCKED` means no safe executable version. No class authorizes REAL-money deployment.

The read-only `report.py` intentionally withholds `PROMISING` on its own because it lacks complete marked-to-market, spread, regime, and operational-quality evidence. A later human review can use the full local broker journal to make that classification with an auditable rationale.
