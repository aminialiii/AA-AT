# Continuous DEMO portfolio policy

The frozen campaign configuration and original S01–S18 strategy rules retain their original SHA-256 identities. `research/demo-live-20261021/risk_policy.json` is a separate, versioned risk-policy overlay. Its effective limits are audited in the local SQLite `risk_policy_versions` table and attached to new intents and reconciled closes. Earlier broker evidence remains under its original configuration and policy provenance.

The portfolio admits at most **five** simultaneous open positions plus pending orders, across all strategies. The maximum aggregate open and pending stop risk is **2% of current verified DEMO equity**. Each proposed order retains the **0.25% of allocated equity** initial-risk cap including a conservative commission, spread, and slippage reserve. The existing 1% daily equity-loss halt, 2% peak-equity drawdown halt, one-position-per-strategy rule, one-position-per-symbol rule, per-order margin cap, DEMO account guard, and emergency stop remain unchanged.

The async worker routes share one admission lock from broker snapshot through durable `PREPARED` intent and broker submission. A single-instance OS lock prevents a second campaign process. Each admission counts broker positions, broker pending orders, and durable reservations. It recalculates position and recognized pending-order stop risk with native broker profit specifications, actual volume and stop, and a fresh cost reserve. Any unknown or unreconciled pending order has unknown risk and suspends new entries until resolved. A signal rejected for full capacity expires according to the existing five-second validity window; it is not held for later execution. The broker connection, identity, exposure, quote, margin, and aggregate limits are rechecked before the sole order-send call.

The shared account was verified as a trade-permitted DEMO hedging account on 2026-10-22. A read-only broker probe observed seven fresh configured symbols, zero pending orders, and positive native minimum-lot margins (roughly USD 7–41 per instrument) against about USD 987 free margin at that observation. The deployed service then restarted HEALTHY with five eligible workers and the new limits visible in its runtime status; its local ledger recorded the policy SHA-256. This is a compatibility observation, not a guarantee of future margin availability. Every proposed order continues to require fresh native margin calculation and the existing free-margin cap. External software submitting to the same account between the final broker snapshot and send cannot be made atomic by this Python service; any unowned exposure detected afterward suspends new routing.

One hundred broker-reconciled fully closed DEMO trades per strategy is the first evaluation milestone, never an execution ceiling. Eligible workers continue on valid signals after 100 unless stopped or suspended by safety policy. The initial 1,800-trade goal assumes all 18 become executable; currently specified and connected workers are fewer. Current performance metrics refresh after each reconciled close. Immutable per-strategy snapshots are written at 100, 200, 500, 1,000, then every further 500 closed trades. Historical research and simulations are excluded from these DEMO reports. The reports include net and gross PnL, expectancy in R, profit factor, closed-trade drawdown, broker costs, fill deviation, a per-trade risk-adjusted ratio, and uncertainty where the sample supports it. Spread is embedded in broker fills but not separately valued in the current closed-trade ledger; intratrade equity and regime attribution remain limitations.

The policy is fail closed: missing policy file, invalid schema, account-type mismatch, unknown broker exposure, stale quotes, uncertain intent, absent native margin, excess portfolio risk, or excess slots prevents new routing. Broker-native protective stops on already opened positions remain in place during service suspension. No policy change alters a strategy's frozen signal rule or authorizes REAL trading.

The market-data supervisor uses the same router lock as the workers when it checks broker exposure, so it cannot mistake a worker's in-flight market order for an unrelated pending order. On restart, an already activated campaign with the same recorded risk policy may wait in SUSPENDED state for a transient stale quote. It performs no new entry until freshness and reconciliation pass. A new campaign, a different policy, an unowned position, or a DEMO-identity failure still fails startup.
